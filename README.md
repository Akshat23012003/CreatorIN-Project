CreatorIN – Creator & Brand Collaboration Platform

📌 Overview

CreatorIN is a full-stack web application designed to connect Creators, Freelancers, and Brands on a single platform. It enables brands to create marketing campaigns, creators to discover collaboration opportunities, freelancers to showcase their skills, and administrators to manage the platform efficiently.

The platform simplifies the influencer marketing and creator economy ecosystem by providing a centralized space for campaign management, applications, networking, and collaboration.
